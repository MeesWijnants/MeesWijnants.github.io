FAQ

Is congressional stock trading legal?

Yes. Members of Congress may own and trade stocks, but they must report each trade above $1,000 within 45 days under the STOCK Act, and trading on non-public information is illegal.

Who trades the most stocks in Congress?

Among the politicians on the Wolf of Washington page, Ro Khanna reports by far the most trades: 3,676 in the last 12 months and more than 27,000 since 2012.

Where can I see Nancy Pelosi's stock trades?

In the official House Clerk filings, or summarized with links to each filing on the Nancy Pelosi page at wolfofwashington.io/congresstrading/nancy-pelosi.
