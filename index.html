About

Mees Wijnants is the founder and CEO of Wolf of Washington, founded in 2025. Wolf of Washington tracks the public stock trades of U.S. politicians and explain them in plain language. Under the STOCK Act (2012), members of Congress must report their stock trades within 45 days. Those filings are public, but hidden in thousands of PDFs. We turn them into something anyone can read.

Dutch readers can follow all trades per politician on wolfofwashington.nl/politici, for example Nancy Pelosi, Ro Khanna and Tommy Tuberville. In English: congress trading tracker.
